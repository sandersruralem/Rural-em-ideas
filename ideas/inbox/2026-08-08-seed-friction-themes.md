## Opportunity statements (jobs)

1. When a watch is issued, help me turn regional hazard info into a one-page “so what for this county” brief.
2. During activation, help agencies share resource/road/shelter truth without a new login or GIS specialist.
3. After windshield surveys, help me produce a same-day county picture (CSV + printable) from phone captures.
4. During public interest spikes, help me publish accurate local updates without living on social media.
5. After operations, help me extract timeline/actions into after-action and grant scraps with minimal re-entry.

## Do not build (yet)

- Full WebEOC-style incident platform
- Real-time CAD integration as an MVP
- AI that makes dispatch or evacuation “decisions”
- Anything requiring every mutual-aid partner to create accounts before use
- Tools that assume a full-time GIS or IT analyst
